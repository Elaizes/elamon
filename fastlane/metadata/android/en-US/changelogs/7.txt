Works with elamond 0.6. A computer paired on one network is found on another: elamond 0.6.1 tells the app where the computer is now (its addresses on the local network and on VPNs such as Tailscale, then its names), and the app tries them all at once when the phone's network changes. Can't reach lists the addresses tried. Settings says where on the computer the phone's profile is. Pairing again keeps the phone's profile. Help installs the service with one command.

Works with elamond 0.6 and later. With elamond 0.7, graphs reach back 1 h, 24 h, 7 days, 30 days, a year or all, and keep their points across restarts. A UPS or an EcoFlow station shows its state, watts and outlets, with a timeline of when it ran on the line or on battery; where the computer allows, its outlets and settings switch from the phone, and an outlet the computer runs from asks for its command to be typed.

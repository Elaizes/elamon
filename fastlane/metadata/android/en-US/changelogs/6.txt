Needs elamond 0.6, which describes every screen: dashboards of widgets set in its settings on the computer (elamond.toml), with the standard Overview, Processes, Sensors and Control among them. The app's look and behaviour come from the profile the computer gives each phone; the first start shows your old settings as a profile to copy there. Actions can take a parameter. Links are set on the computer only. Pairings stay.
